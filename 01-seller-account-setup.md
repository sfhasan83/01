## Phase 0 — Before you sign up (1–3 days)

| # | Step | Who |
|---|------|-----|
| 0.1 | Decide your business model. **Recommended for a beginner: Private Label or Wholesale using FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon).** Avoid dropshipping from other retailers (Amazon bans it). | 🤝 |
| 0.2 | Decide **Individual** ($0.99 per item sold) vs **Professional** ($39.99/month) plan. Choose Professional once you expect >40 sales/month; it unlocks ads, coupons, deals, Brand Registry tools and bulk listing. | 🤝 |
| 0.3 | Register a business (optional but recommended): an LLC in the US, or a sole proprietorship / company in your country. Non-US sellers can sell on amazon.com without a US company. | 👤 |
| 0.4 | Get a dedicated business email and phone number you will keep long term. | 👤 |
| 0.5 | Set aside starting capital. A realistic first private-label order is **$2,000–$5,000** (inventory + samples + photos + first month of ads). | 👤 |

## Phase 1 — Gather your documents

Have these ready **before** you start; mismatched details are the #1 reason accounts get stuck in verification.

- 👤 Government photo ID (passport or national ID / driver's license), not expired, name exactly as on your bank account
- 👤 Recent bank or credit-card statement, or utility bill (last 180 days) showing the **same name and address**
- 👤 Chargeable international credit card (Visa / Mastercard) for the monthly fee
- 👤 Bank account that can receive USD payouts (a US account, or a service such as Payoneer / Wise / Amazon Currency Converter for Sellers)
- 👤 Tax ID: US SSN or EIN for US persons; non-US sellers complete the **W-8BEN** (individual) or **W-8BEN-E** (business) interview
- 👤 Business registration documents (if registering as a company)

## Phase 2 — Create the Seller Central account (about 1 hour + 1–3 weeks verification)

1. 👤 Go to **sell.amazon.com** → *Sign up*. Use the business email from step 0.4 (not your personal shopping account if you can avoid it).
2. 👤 Enter business type, legal name and address exactly as on your documents.
3. 👤 Enter your ID and personal details.
4. 👤 Add the credit card and deposit (bank) account.
5. 👤 Complete the **tax interview** (W-9 or W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E).
6. 👤 Enter store name (you can change it later) and answer product questions (UPC/GTIN, brand ownership).
7. 👤 **Identity verification**: upload documents and complete the **video call** or in-person verification with Amazon. Have the original documents in hand.
8. 👤 Verify your address via the postcard / code Amazon sends (if requested).

> ⚠️ Open **only one** seller account per person/business. Duplicate accounts get suspended.

## Phase 3 — Set up your shop properly (week 1–2 after approval)

| # | Step | Who |
|---|------|-----|
| 3.1 | Turn on **two-step verification** and add a backup phone. | 👤 |
| 3.2 | Fill out *Settings → Account Info*: return address, shipping settings, business hours, notifications. | 👤 |
| 3.3 | Enrol in **FBA** (*Settings → Fulfillment by Amazon*). | 👤 |
| 3.4 | Choose your first product using the daily report (see `daily-reports/`). Run the numbers in Amazon's **Revenue Calculator** before ordering. | 🤝 |
| 3.5 | File a **trademark** for your brand name (USPTO, ~$350 per class). Use **Amazon IP Accelerator** to get Brand Registry while the trademark is pending. | 👤 (I can draft the brand name shortlist) |

| 3.8 | Source the product: request samples from 3–5 suppliers (Alibaba, Global Sources, or local manufacturers). Check quality yourself before a bulk order. Use a third-party inspection for orders over ~$3,000. | 👤 (I can write supplier messages and compare quotes) |

## Key costs to budget (US, 2026)

- Professional plan: **$39.99/month**
- Referral fee: **~15%** of sale price in most categories (8% in some categories for low-priced items; check Amazon's fee schedule for your category)
- FBA fulfilment fee: roughly **$3.50–$6.50 per unit** for a small, light standard-size item; more for larger items. Peak-season surcharge applies 15 Oct – 14 Jan.
- Monthly storage fee, plus an inbound placement fee depending on how you ship
- Advertising: plan **$15–$30/day** during launch
- Rule of thumb: **landed product cost should be ≤ 25% of your selling price** to leave a healthy margin after Amazon fees and ads.

## Rules that will get you suspended (avoid these)

- Paying for reviews, giving free products in exchange for reviews, or asking only happy customers to review
- Reviews from friends and family
- Inserts asking for a 5-star review
- Selling restricted / gated products without approval (supplements, toys in Q4, topicals, etc. may need extra approval or testing)
- Using another brand's trademark in your listing
- Multiple seller accounts
