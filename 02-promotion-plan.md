# Shop Promotion Plan: US, UAE and Saudi Arabia

Sections 1–7 apply to all three stores (written with US examples). **Section 8** covers what to change for Amazon.ae and Amazon.sa.

Goal: get the first product to **steady daily sales with a healthy ad cost (TACoS under ~15%) within 90 days.**

All tactics below are allowed under Amazon's policies (as of November 2026). Anything that pays for, filters, or incentivises reviews is **not** allowed and is excluded.

---

## 1. Before launch: make the listing convert

Ads only buy clicks. The listing decides whether those clicks become sales.

- [ ] **Main image**: pure white background, product fills 85% of frame, high resolution (zoomable at 1600px+)
- [ ] **6 more images**: lifestyle in use, size/scale image, infographic of 3 key benefits, what's in the box, comparison vs. generic alternative, close-up of quality details
- [ ] **Video** (30–60 s, phone-shot is fine)
- [ ] **Title**: Brand + main keyword + 2–3 key features + size/quantity. Under 200 characters
- [ ] **5 bullet points**: lead each with the benefit in caps, then the proof
- [ ] **Backend search terms**: synonyms, misspellings, Spanish terms; no repeats, no competitor brands
- [ ] **A+ Content** (needs Brand Registry): comparison chart linking your other products
- [ ] **Price**: within the price band of the top 10 sellers, ideally ending in .99 or .97
- [ ] Ask 5 people to look at the listing for 5 seconds and tell you what the product is and why to buy it

## 2. Amazon PPC (pay-per-click) launch plan

### Week 1–2: Discovery
| Campaign | Type | Daily budget | Starting bid |
|---|---|---|---|
| SP – Auto | Sponsored Products, automatic targeting | $10 | Amazon suggested bid |
| SP – Exact | 5–10 top keywords, exact match | $10 | Suggested bid +20% |
| SP – Broad | Same keywords, broad match | $5 | Suggested bid |

- Set campaign bidding to **"Dynamic bids – down only"**.
- Target **launch ACoS of 40–60%**. You are buying sales history and ranking, not profit, in the first 2 weeks.

### Week 3–6: Optimise (every 3–4 days)
1. Download the **Search Term Report**.
2. Search terms with **2+ orders** → move to the Exact campaign.
3. Search terms with **15+ clicks and 0 orders** → add as **negative exact**.
4. Keywords with ACoS under target → raise bid 10–15%. Over target → lower bid 10–15%.
5. Add a **Product Targeting** campaign aimed at competitor ASINs that have worse reviews or higher prices than you.

### Week 7+: Scale
- Turn on **Sponsored Brands** (headline banner + video) once Brand Registry is active.
- Turn on **Sponsored Display** retargeting to people who viewed your product but did not buy.
- Target ACoS: your **break-even ACoS = profit margin before ads**. Aim to settle 5–10 points below it.

## 3. Deals and coupons

| Tool | When to use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Coupon** (5–20% off) | From day 1 | Shows an orange badge in search results; you pay a small fee per redemption. Most cost-effective launch tool. |
| **Prime Exclusive Discount** | After 2–4 weeks | Shows a strikethrough price to Prime members. |
| **Lightning Deal** | When you have 3+ star rating and enough stock | Time-limited, fee applies; drives a big sales spike that lifts organic rank. |
| **Best Deal** | Big events (Prime Day, Black Friday, Cyber Monday) | Needs to be booked weeks ahead. |
| **Social Media Promo Code** | When you run social/influencer campaigns | Creates a shareable landing page and code you can track. |

> ⚠️ Peak dates: Amazon's **Black Friday Week runs 19–30 November 2026**, and FBA arrival deadlines for it have passed. A new seller should plan the first big push for **January (New Year: fitness, organisation, wellness)** and **Valentine's Day**.

## 4. Getting reviews within Amazon's rules

1. **Amazon Vine** (Brand Registry required): enrol up to 30 units; trusted reviewers get the product free and write honest reviews. Best way to get your first 5–30 reviews.
2. **"Request a Review" button** in Seller Central for every order, 5–30 days after delivery (or automate it with a tool such as Helium 10 Follow-Up / Jungle Scout).
3. **Product insert** that thanks the customer, gives usage tips and support contact. It may say "leave a review" but must **not** ask for a positive review or offer anything in return.
4. **Excellent customer service**: answer buyer messages within 24 hours, fix problems fast. This prevents negative reviews.
5. ❌ Never: pay for reviews, give refunds/gifts for reviews, ask friends/family, or only ask happy customers.

## 5. Social media and external traffic

External traffic helps Amazon rank you higher, and with Brand Registry you earn the **Brand Referral Bonus (around 10% of sales from external traffic)** when you use **Amazon Attribution** links.

| Channel | What to post | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | 15–30 s "problem → product solves it" videos, unboxing, before/after. Join TikTok Shop if your category fits. | 1 per day |
| **Instagram Reels** | Same videos re-cut; product in lifestyle settings | 4–5 per week |
| **Pinterest** | Product pins linking to your Amazon listing (great for home, kitchen, beauty, pet) | 5 pins per week |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Reuse TikTok videos | 3 per week |
| **Facebook groups** | Join niche groups (e.g. pet owners, home organisation); give helpful advice, don't spam | Weekly |

### Influencers
- Start with **micro-influencers (5k–50k followers)** in your niche. Send free product; pay a flat fee ($50–$300) for a video if needed.
- Give each influencer their own **Amazon Attribution link** or **Social Media Promo Code** to measure results.
- Join the **Amazon Creator Connections** program (in Seller Central, Brand Registry) so Amazon influencers promote your product for a commission.

### Email list
- Put a small QR-code card in the box offering a free guide/recipe e-book or extended warranty (not tied to reviews) to collect customer emails for future launches.

## 6. Weekly routine

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Read the daily product report; check orders, messages, stock; post one TikTok/Reel | 30 min |
| Mon & Thu | PPC optimisation (search term report, bids, negatives) | 45 min |
| Wednesday | Check reviews, answer questions, update images/A+ if conversion rate is under 10% | 30 min |
| Friday | Inventory planning: reorder when stock = 60 days of sales + supplier lead time | 20 min |
| Monthly | Calculate profit per unit, TACoS, return rate; decide on next product | 1 hr |

## 7. Key metrics to track

| Metric | Healthy target |
|---|---|
| Conversion rate (Unit Session %) | 10–20% |
| ACoS (launch) | 40–60% |
| ACoS (steady) | Under break-even margin |
| TACoS (ad spend / total sales) | Under 10–15% |
| Star rating | 4.3+ |
| Return rate | Under 5% (category dependent) |
| Inventory | Never out of stock; 45–90 days of cover |

## 8. UAE and Saudi Arabia: what to do differently

### 8.1 Listings
- Write every listing in **English and Arabic** (Amazon.sa shoppers search mostly in Arabic; Amazon.ae is mixed). Use Arabic keywords in backend search terms, including common spellings in Gulf dialect and Arabizi (e.g. "عباية", "مبخرة", "mabkhara").
- Show prices and sizes in local units (AED / SAR, cm, ml). Use images with Gulf-relevant lifestyle settings (majlis, villa kitchens, Ramadan table).
- Make sure your product is **Prime eligible via FBA**: fast delivery (often same/next day in Dubai, Riyadh, Jeddah) is the main reason Gulf shoppers choose Amazon over other sites.

### 8.2 Ads (PPC)
- Use the same campaign structure as Section 2, with **separate budgets per store**. Do not split evenly: Amazon.sa usually has **cheaper clicks and fewer competitors**; Amazon.ae is more competitive.
- Launch budget guide: **AED 40–80/day** on Amazon.ae, **SAR 40–80/day** on Amazon.sa.
- Run **separate Arabic-keyword and English-keyword campaigns** so you can see which language converts.
- Raise bids in the **evening (after 8 pm)**, and during Ramadan **after Iftar until 2 am**, when Gulf shoppers are most active. Use Amazon's dayparting / schedule rules where available.

### 8.4 Social media in the Gulf
| Channel | Why | What to post |
|---|---|---|
| **Snapchat** | Very high usage in Saudi Arabia, especially 18–35 | Short how-to videos, behind-the-scenes, Snap ads with "Shop on Amazon" link |
| **TikTok** | Huge in KSA and UAE | Arabic voice-over product demos, Ramadan prep videos |
| **Instagram** | Main channel for UAE; strong in KSA | Reels, Stories with Amazon links, UGC from customers |
| **X (Twitter)** | Popular in KSA for news and trends | Deal announcements around White Friday, Ramadan |
| **WhatsApp Business** | How Gulf customers prefer to ask questions | Catalogue + Channel for repeat customers (send them back to Amazon to buy) |

- **Influencers:** UAE requires paid influencers to hold an advertiser permit (UAE Media Council); in KSA, influencers need a **Mawthooq** licence. Work only with licensed influencers and ask for their licence number.
- Use **Amazon Attribution** links (where available in your store) or a Social Media Promo Code to measure what each influencer brings.

### 8.5 Reviews and trust
- **Amazon Vine** and the **Request a Review** button work on Amazon.ae and Amazon.sa as in the US, once Brand Registry is active in that store.
- Gulf shoppers rely heavily on **cash on delivery** and easy returns; FBA handles both. Keep an Arabic-speaking contact for buyer messages.
- The same review rules apply: no paid, incentivised or filtered review requests.
