## 4. Top pick deep-dive: dog snuffle mat / puzzle feeder

- **Product idea:** Machine-washable snuffle mat with **non-slip base and adjustable difficulty** (detachable pieces), sold as a size range (S/M/L) or bundled with a lick mat.
- **Unit economics (example at $29.99):**

  | Item | Amount |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | $29.99 |
  | Referral fee (15%) | −$4.50 |
  | FBA fee (small standard / large standard, ~1 lb, estimate) | −$5.50 |
  | Landed cost (product + freight + duty, estimate) | −$6.50 |
  | Ads (~15% of sales) | −$4.50 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ $9.00 (≈ 30%)** |

- **Differentiation angle:** Most low-cost mats shed fabric and slide on the floor. Lead with "won't slide, won't shred, goes in the washing machine", show a slow-motion video of a dog using it, and offer sizes for big dogs.
- **Validate before ordering:**
  1. Top 10 results for "snuffle mat for dogs": aim for a median under ~400 reviews.
  2. Read 1–3 star reviews of the top 5 and list the top 3 complaints; your product must fix them.
  3. Check search volume (Brand Analytics, Helium 10 or Jungle Scout): 3,000+ searches/month for the main keyword.
  4. Put the exact dimensions and weight into the Amazon Revenue Calculator.
- **Risks:** Low entry barrier means copycats; build a brand (A+ content, bundle, multiple sizes). Avoid copying patented designs.
