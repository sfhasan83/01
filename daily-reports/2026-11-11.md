## 4. Top pick deep-dives

### 🇺🇸 Dog snuffle mat
Unchanged from [yesterday's report](2026-11-10.md#4-top-pick-deep-dive-dog-snuffle-mat--puzzle-feeder): ~$9 net profit per unit at $29.99 (≈30%). Differentiate with a non-slip, machine-washable design in three sizes.

### 🇦🇪🇸🇦 Ramadan hosting set
- **Product idea:** A gift-boxed **dates & sweets serving set** (tray + 3 bowls + lid) in a modern Ramadan design, or a **Ramadan table runner + 2 battery LED lanterns** bundle. Gift-ready box with Arabic + English text.
- **Unit economics (example at AED 89 on Amazon.ae):**

  | Item | AED |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 89.00 |
  | Referral fee (home ~15%) | −13.35 |
  | FBA fee (standard, ~1 kg, estimate) | −12.00 |
  | Landed cost (product + freight + 5% duty + 5% import VAT, estimate) | −24.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −13.35 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ AED 26 (≈ 29%)** |

  Amazon.sa is similar at SAR 89–99, but budget for 15% import VAT (reclaimable once VAT registered) and SABER if the set includes anything electrical.
- **Differentiation angle:** Most listings are plain plastic or generic décor shipped in a brown box. Lead with **"gift-ready, arrives before Iftar"** (Prime), premium finish and a matching family of products (tray, runner, lantern) for cross-selling.
- **Validate before ordering:** search "رمضان" / "ramadan decor" / "dates tray" on Amazon.ae and Amazon.sa; note top 10 prices and review counts (aim for a median under ~300); check that the 1–3 star reviews complain about breakage or cheap finish you can fix.
- **Risks:** **One season per year.** Unsold stock waits 11 months (or sell it for Eid al-Adha / weddings). Order a modest first batch (200–300 units). Battery LED items may need a conformity certificate; plain trays and runners usually do not.
