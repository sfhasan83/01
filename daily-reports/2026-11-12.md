### 🆕 Car detailing kit (all three stores)
- **Product idea:** 12–16 piece kit in a carry bag: plush microfiber towels (different GSM for drying, glass, interior), wheel and detail brushes, wash mitt, foam applicator pads. **No chemicals**, so no hazmat review and no SABER/SFDA chemical approvals.
- **Unit economics (example at $29.99 on Amazon.com):**

  | Item | Amount |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | $29.99 |
  | Referral fee (automotive ~12%) | −$3.60 |
  | FBA fee (large standard, ~1.5 lb, estimate) | −$6.20 |
  | Landed cost (estimate) | −$6.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −$4.50 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ $9.70 (≈ 32%)** |

  In the Gulf, sell at **AED/SAR 89–109**; add 5% duty + import VAT to the landed cost.
- **Differentiation angle:** Gulf heat and dust: market it as a **"dust & sand kit"** with an interior dashboard brush and a sunshade-friendly glass towel. In the US, position it as a gift for car lovers (Father's Day, Christmas) and a **"ceramic coating maintenance kit"** to ride the ceramic-polish search trend.
- **Validate before ordering:** top 10 for "car detailing kit" / "microfiber car wash kit" (aim for a median under ~500 reviews), most common complaints (lint, towels shrinking after washing), and the GSM of competitors' towels.
- **Risks:** Big brands (Chemical Guys, Meguiar's) own the liquid side; stay in accessories. Towel quality varies widely between suppliers, so wash-test samples.
