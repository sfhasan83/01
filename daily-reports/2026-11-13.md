### 🆕 Oud / bakhoor-scented car air freshener (Amazon.ae, Amazon.sa)
- **Product idea:** A **3-pack of hanging fragrance cards or vent clips** in Gulf scents (oud, bakhoor, musk, rose-oud) with premium Arabic/English packaging. Add a refill pack later for repeat sales.
- **Unit economics (example: 3-pack at AED 39 on Amazon.ae):**

  | Item | AED |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 39.00 |
  | Referral fee (~10%) | −3.90 |
  | FBA fee (small, light, estimate) | −7.00 |
  | Landed cost (incl. 5% duty + 5% import VAT, estimate) | −6.50 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −5.85 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ AED 15.75 (≈ 40%)** |

  Amazon.sa: similar at SAR 39–45; budget for 15% import VAT.
- **Differentiation angle:** The top sellers (Godrej, Little Trees) sell Western scents at AED 5–21 with 3.8–4.1 star ratings; the most common complaint for this kind of product is that the scent fades in days. Lead with **"long-lasting oud, 30+ days"** and luxury packaging that works as a small gift.
- **Validate before ordering:** search "معطر سيارة عود" and "oud car freshener" on both stores; test how long 3 scent suppliers last in a hot car (Gulf cars reach 60°C+ in summer).
- **Risks:** **Liquid or oil-based fresheners can be classed as dangerous goods** (flammable), which complicates FBA. Choose **dry hanging cards or solid gel** and ask the supplier for an SDS (safety data sheet). Heat can make some gels melt or leak; test before ordering.
