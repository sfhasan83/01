### ⬆️ Premium windshield sunshade (Amazon.sa, Amazon.ae)
- **Product idea:** A **heat-reflective umbrella-style or multi-layer foldable sunshade** offered in **sizes by car type** (sedan / SUV / pickup), with a fit chart for the top Gulf models (Toyota Land Cruiser, Camry, Hilux, Nissan Patrol, Hyundai). Optional bundle: sunshade + heat-proof phone mount.
- **Unit economics (example at SAR 59 on Amazon.sa):**

  | Item | SAR |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 59.00 |
  | Referral fee (~10–12%, check automotive rate) | −6.50 |
  | FBA fee (standard, ~0.8 kg, estimate) | −10.00 |
  | Landed cost (product + freight + ~5% duty + 15% import VAT, estimate) | −15.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −8.85 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ SAR 18.65 (≈ 32%)** |

  Amazon.ae: similar at AED 49–69 (5% import VAT instead of 15%).
- **Differentiation angle:** The SAR 17 leader is rated 3.3★, so buyers clearly accept a sunshade but are unhappy with it. Fix the usual complaints: **exact fit by model**, thicker reflective layers (show a thermometer test video: "cabin 15°C cooler"), strong frame that doesn't sag, and a storage pouch. Price at 3× the cheap leader and justify it in the main image.
- **Validate before ordering:** confirm the top 3 complaints in 1–3★ reviews, check how many sunshade listings rate 4.3★+ (fewer = better for you), and order samples from 3 suppliers and test them in a parked car at midday.
- **Risks:** Peak demand is **April–September** (Gulf summer), so launch by March. Large items can fall into a bigger FBA size tier; keep the folded size small. Umbrella-style designs may have patents in some markets; ask your supplier about design rights.
