### ⬆️ AirTag holders (all three stores, Gulf first)
- **Product idea:** A small range made for the **AirTag 2nd Gen** (and compatible with 1st Gen): (1) waterproof dog-collar holder, (2) luggage tag holder, (3) slim wallet card holder. Sell as single items and as a **3-pack "travel set"**.
- **Unit economics (examples):**

  | Item | 🇺🇸 3-pack at $15.99 | 🇦🇪 3-pack at AED 49 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Referral fee | −$2.40 (15%) | −AED 4.90 (~10%) |
  | FBA fee (small standard, estimate) | −$3.30 | −AED 7.00 |
  | Landed cost (incl. Gulf duty + import VAT, estimate) | −$2.50 | −AED 8.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −$2.40 | −AED 7.35 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ $5.40 (≈ 34%)** | **≈ AED 21.75 (≈ 44%)** |

- **Differentiation angle:** Say **"fits AirTag 2nd Gen"** in the title and main image, and show the fit with a short video. For the Gulf: Arabic listing, gift-ready box, and colours that suit car keys and abaya bags. Bundle the 3-pack as a travel set before the **Eid and summer travel** season.
- **Validate before ordering:** check whether 2nd Gen dimensions differ from 1st Gen (order one AirTag 2nd Gen and test the samples). Search both Gulf stores for listings that name 2nd Gen.
- **Risks:** Don't use Apple's logo or "Apple" in the brand name; "for AirTag" is the usual wording. The product is cheap and easy to copy, so build a range and collect reviews early.
