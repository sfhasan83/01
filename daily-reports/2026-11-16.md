### 🆕 Eid & party balloon kit (Amazon.ae, Amazon.sa)
- **Product idea:** An all-in-one **"Eid Mubarak" kit**: foil letter banner, 30–40 latex and foil balloons (gold/white/green), crescent and star foil balloons, ribbon, glue dots and a hand pump. Make a **birthday version** in the same box format for year-round sales.
- **Unit economics (example at AED 39 on Amazon.ae):**

  | Item | AED |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 39.00 |
  | Referral fee (~10%, check party-supplies rate) | −3.90 |
  | FBA fee (small standard, light, estimate) | −7.00 |
  | Landed cost (incl. 5% duty + 5% import VAT, estimate) | −7.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −5.85 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ AED 15.25 (≈ 39%)** |

- **Differentiation angle:** Most top sellers are plain coloured balloon bags. A **themed, complete kit** ("everything you need to decorate in 20 minutes") with Arabic + English packaging and a photo of the finished wall wins on both convenience and gifting. Cross-sell with the Ramadan table set ("Ramadan & Eid bundle").
- **Validate before ordering:** search "عيد مبارك بالونات" and "eid decorations" on both stores: note prices, review counts and whether kits or loose balloons lead.
- **Risks:** **Seasonal spikes** (Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha ~mid-May 2027); the birthday version smooths sales. Latex balloons carry a choking warning for children under 8; print it on the pack. Avoid electric pumps (they need an electrical certificate); include a hand pump.
