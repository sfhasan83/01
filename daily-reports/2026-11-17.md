### ⬆️ Squeeze & stress-relief toys (Amazon.sa, Amazon.ae), as product #2
- **Product idea:** A **3-pack of slow-rise squeeze toys** in food shapes (butter block, dates, kunafa or cookie shapes for a local twist), made from non-toxic TPR, in a gift box. Position it for **teens and adults (14+)** as stress relief. Kids still buy it, but the age marking is important.
- **Unit economics (example: 3-pack at SAR 39 on Amazon.sa):**

  | Item | SAR |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 39.00 |
  | Referral fee (~10–15%, check toys rate) | −5.00 |
  | FBA fee (small standard, estimate) | −7.00 |
  | Landed cost (product + freight + duty + 15% import VAT, estimate) | −7.50 |
  | Certification (SABER + lab test, ~SAR 3,000–6,000 spread over 1,000 units) | −4.50 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −5.85 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ SAR 9.15 (≈ 23%)** in the first batch, rising to ~35% after certification is paid off |

- **Differentiation angle:** The leaders are rated 2.6–3.9★; the usual complaints for this kind of toy are tearing, stickiness, a chemical smell and not rising slowly. Lead with **"tear-resistant, non-sticky, no smell, tested safe"** and show the certificate mark.
- **Validate before ordering:** read the 1–3★ reviews of the top 3 squeeze toys on both stores; confirm whether your supplier has **EN 71 / ASTM F963** test reports; get a SABER cost quote from a certification agent.
- **Risks:** **Viral toy trends can fade within 6–12 months.** Order a small first batch (300–500 units). Toys without SABER certificates are rejected at Saudi customs. Never claim "for babies" or "under 3".
