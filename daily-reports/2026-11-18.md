### 🆕 Compact UV sun umbrella (Amazon.ae, Amazon.sa)
- **Product idea:** A **UPF 50+ auto open/close compact umbrella** with a black UV-blocking inner coating, under 350 g, in a slim case. Market it for **sun first, rain second**: walking to the car, school runs, Hajj/Umrah, outdoor events.
- **Unit economics (example at AED 59 on Amazon.ae):**

  | Item | AED |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 59.00 |
  | Referral fee (~15% fashion/luggage) | −8.85 |
  | FBA fee (standard, ~0.4 kg, estimate) | −9.00 |
  | Landed cost (incl. 5% duty + 5% import VAT, estimate) | −14.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −8.85 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ AED 18.30 (≈ 31%)** |

- **Differentiation angle:** The leader is a generic rain umbrella at AED 23.50 with 4.0★. Show a **thermometer test** ("up to 10°C cooler underneath"), UPF 50+ test certificate, and Gulf use cases (Umrah, school pickup). Offer a matching **travel bundle** with the packing organiser set.
- **Validate before ordering:** search "مظلة شمس" and "UV umbrella" on both stores; read the 1–3★ reviews of the top 3 (common complaints for umbrellas: frame breaks, auto button fails); ask suppliers for a UPF test report.
- **Risks:** Peak demand is **April–September**; launch by March. Umbrellas break easily in wind, so pay for a stronger fibreglass frame.
