### 🆕 Moisture absorbers (Amazon.ae, Amazon.sa)
- **Product idea:** **Hanging closet dehumidifier bags** (calcium chloride, ~200 g each) in a 6- or 10-pack, with a light fragrance option (oud or unscented). Add a **refill subscription** with Amazon Subscribe & Save once eligible.
- **Unit economics (10-pack at AED 59 on Amazon.ae):**

  | Item | AED |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 59.00 |
  | Referral fee (~15% home) | −8.85 |
  | FBA fee (standard, ~2.3 kg, estimate) | −15.00 |
  | Landed cost (heavy, so freight is higher; incl. duty + VAT, estimate) | −14.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −8.85 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ AED 12.30 (≈ 21%)** |

  The margin is thinner because of weight. A **6-pack at AED 39** may convert better and cost less to ship.
- **Differentiation angle:** Show how much water it collects (a "before/after" jar photo after 30 days in a Dubai closet); offer **leak-proof** bags (the main complaint for this type of product is leaking onto clothes); Arabic labelling for abaya and wardrobe use.
- **Validate before ordering:** check whether calcium chloride packs need extra paperwork for FBA in each store (they are usually not dangerous goods, but ask for an SDS); compare top 10 prices and pack sizes; source locally in the UAE if possible to cut freight on a heavy product.
- **Risks:** Heavy for its price; a local or regional supplier makes the numbers better. Leaking bags cause bad reviews, so test samples for a month.
