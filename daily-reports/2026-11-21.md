### 🆕 Cat accessories (Amazon.sa, Amazon.ae)
- **Product idea:** Lead with a **double-layer honeycomb cat litter mat** (traps litter, waterproof, folds open to empty), 60×45 cm and 75×55 cm. Follow with **corrugated cardboard scratcher boards** (refill packs = repeat sales) and a collapsible **cat tunnel**.
- **Unit economics (litter mat at SAR 59 on Amazon.sa):**

  | Item | SAR |
  |---|---|
  | Sale price | 59.00 |
  | Referral fee (pet: ~8% up to SAR 50 then 15%, estimate) | −7.50 |
  | FBA fee (standard, ~0.6 kg folded, estimate) | −10.00 |
  | Landed cost (incl. duty + 15% import VAT, estimate) | −13.00 |
  | Ads (~15%) | −8.85 |
  | **Net profit / unit** | **≈ SAR 19.65 (≈ 33%)** |

- **Differentiation angle:** Arabic listing and a **video of litter falling through the honeycomb** layer; sizes for large litter boxes; colours that suit Gulf homes (beige, grey). Bundle the mat + scratcher as a **"new cat starter kit"**.
- **Validate before ordering:** search "دواسة رمل قطط" (cat litter mat) and "cat scratcher" on Amazon.sa and Amazon.ae; note top 10 prices and review counts; read 1–3★ reviews (common complaints for litter mats: litter stuck in the middle layer, mat curling at the edges, smell from cheap PVC).
- **Risks:** Low entry barrier, so others will copy you; build a small range fast. Cardboard scratchers are bulky for their price; use flat-pack or compressed designs to stay in a small FBA size tier.
