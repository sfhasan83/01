## 5. Ready-to-send supplier enquiry (Ramadan hosting set)

Copy this into Alibaba, Made-in-China or an email to a UAE/Saudi wholesaler. Send it to **3–5 suppliers** so you can compare prices.

> **Subject:** Enquiry: Ramadan serving set, private label, Amazon FBA (UAE/KSA)
>
> Hello,
>
> I am launching a home brand on Amazon UAE and Saudi Arabia and I'm interested in your Ramadan serving / dates tray sets.
>
> Please send me:
> 1. Price per unit for **300, 500 and 1,000 units**, and your MOQ.
> 2. Options for **custom logo** printing and a **gift box** with Arabic + English text.
> 3. Available finishes and colours (gold, white, wood, acrylic).
> 4. Unit size and weight when packed, and carton size / units per carton.
> 5. Production time and earliest ship date. **I need goods delivered to Dubai (or Jeddah) by 10 January 2027.**
> 6. Price for **1–2 samples** shipped to [your city], and sample lead time.
> 7. Any test reports (food-contact safety for trays/bowls, e.g. LFGB or FDA).
>
> Can you also quote a matching **Ramadan table runner** and **battery LED lantern** (optional bundle)?
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name] · [Brand name] · [WhatsApp / email]

Tips: ask for **DDP** (delivered duty paid) prices to Dubai or Jeddah so customs is handled; pay for samples by card or PayPal for protection; never pay 100% up front for bulk orders (30% deposit, 70% after inspection is standard).
