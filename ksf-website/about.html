Our Story

Born in Lahore.
Made for Pakistan.

KSF Food Industries was founded with one simple idea: Pakistan deserves world-class snacks with flavours that taste like home.

Factory photo can go here
Who we are

A Lahori snack company with big ambitions.

From our factory in Lahore, KSF Food Industries slices, fries, seasons and packs potato chips and corn pops for shops, marts and snack lovers across Pakistan.

Our promise is right there in our name: Crunch You Can Trust. Real potatoes, quality corn, no artificial colours, no preservatives and 100% vegetarian recipes. The result is real taste and real crunch, unmistakably KSF.

Our goal is simple: to become the snack Pakistan reaches for first, and to carry the taste of Lahore to the world.

What we stand for

Three promises.

Quality first

Made with real potatoes and quality corn. 100% vegetarian, with no artificial colours and no preservatives added.

Flavour of home

Seasonings developed for Pakistani taste, from mild and classic to proper desi heat.

Fresh to shelf

Our Pops are baked, not fried, and every pack is sealed fresh so it tastes just-made.

Every pack carries the taste of Lahore.
