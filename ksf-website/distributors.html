Distributor FAQ

Your questions.

Who can become a KSF distributor?

Any registered trader, wholesaler or distribution company in Pakistan with a delivery vehicle, storage space and an existing network of shops can apply. Retailers and marts can also buy cartons directly.

Which cities and areas are open?

We are appointing distributors in Lahore first, then across Punjab and the rest of Pakistan. Tell us your city and the areas you cover, and we will confirm if the territory is open.

What do distributors get from KSF?

A protected territory, dependable supply from our Lahore factory, trade pricing, point-of-sale material, launch promotions and social media support in your area.

How fast will KSF reply?

We aim to reply within one working day. For the fastest answer, WhatsApp our sales team on 0321-6806097.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Yes. Serious trade partners can ask for a sample carton of our chips and pops before placing a first order.

Does KSF do private label or export?

Yes. Send us your quantities, pack sizes and target market and our sales team will share options.
