Farm to crunch

How a KSF chip is born.

Every pack leaves our Lahore factory through the same four careful steps, so the crunch you love is the crunch you get.

Pick

Real potatoes for our chips and quality corn for our pops. Nothing fake.

Slice

Potatoes are washed and crinkle-cut; corn is puffed and baked, not fried.

Season

Tumbled in seasonings made for Pakistani taste, with no artificial colours.

Seal

Packed fresh with no preservatives added, 100% vegetarian.
